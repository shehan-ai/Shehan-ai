## About

I am a founder building practical AI products, companies, and selective open-source tools.

I lead [Kaimera X](https://kaimerax.com/), an AI-first software and solutions company that builds business automations, custom software, and AI-powered applications around how organizations actually work. I also lead [AI RedCell](https://www.airedcell.dev/), a free learning and research platform focused on practical AI security and safer AI systems.

## Research principles

I believe technology should be useful, understandable, and responsible.

- Start with a real problem, not a fashionable technology.
- Build the simplest useful version first.
- Make systems secure and understandable.
- Share knowledge and tools when doing so is safe and useful.

<details>
<summary>How I build</summary>

1. Start with a real problem.
2. Understand the people and workflow around it.
3. Build the simplest useful version.
4. Make it secure, understandable, and measurable.
5. Improve it through feedback.

</details>
